Frequently asked questions

Slack is a new way to communicate with your team. It’s faster, better organized, and more secure than email. It’s your digital HQ.

Email is the most common way to communicate in business, but it’s not designed for the way we work today. Slack brings communication, collaboration, and tools together in one place.

Slack has a free version for small teams. We also offer paid plans with more features, storage, and support. Check our pricing page for more details.

You can use Slack on the web, on your computer, and on your phone. You can create channels for topics, projects, or teams, and message anyone directly.
